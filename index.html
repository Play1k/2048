About this game

This is play1k's own version of 2048, built from scratch. It's smooth on phones, tablets and laptops, with arrow keys, swipe, or mouse drag controls.

The rules are simple: slide the tiles, and matching numbers merge into one. Try to reach 2048 — or keep going past it and see how far you get.

Your score, best score, and leaderboard are saved right in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. It's just for you.
